1. Platform Purpose

AgriCore is an agricultural intelligence platform designed to assist farmers, government officials, and agricultural organisations in South Africa. The platform provides tools for farm performance tracking, support plan management, analytics, and farmer data management.

AgriCore is intended for authorised personnel within South African agricultural departments and registered farmers only. By using this platform, you acknowledge its intended scope and purpose.

2. Laws & Compliance (POPIA)

AgriCore is committed to complying with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) of South Africa. We process your personal information lawfully and for legitimate purposes only.

Your Rights Under POPIA

  • Right to access: You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to correction: You may request that incorrect or outdated information be updated.
  • Right to deletion: You may request the deletion of your personal information, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Right to object: You may object to the processing of your personal information in certain circumstances.
  • Right to complain: You may lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator if you believe your rights have been infringed.

Lawful Basis for Processing

We process personal information on the following bases: consent given during registration, legitimate interest for improving agricultural outcomes, compliance with legal obligations under agricultural regulations, and performance of a contract or public task related to government agricultural services.

4. Data Usage & Privacy

What Data We Collect

  • Personal information: Name, email address, phone number, home address, role.
  • Farm data: Farm name, size, type, province, district, crop/livestock records.
  • Performance data: Yield history, production records, performance scores.
  • Verification documents: Identity documents and farm registration documents.
  • Platform usage: Activity logs, login times, and feature usage for platform improvement.

How We Use Your Data

  • Providing personalised dashboards, analytics, and support plans.
  • Generating reports and insights for agricultural decision-making.
  • Verifying user identity and farm ownership for data integrity.
  • Communicating important notifications about your account and farm data.
  • Aggregated (non-personal) statistics for national agricultural planning.

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption and access controls. We use Supabase for data storage with Row Level Security (RLS) policies to ensure users can only access authorised data. Sensitive data such as passwords are never stored in plain text. All API communications are encrypted.

We will never sell your personal information to third parties. Data sharing with government agencies is limited to the scope of authorised agricultural support programmes.

5. Account Management

Account Creation

To use AgriCore, you must create an account with a valid email address and strong password. Accounts are assigned roles (farmer, government, admin, etc.) that determine access levels.

Account Deletion

You may request account deletion through the Settings panel in your dashboard. Deletion requests require password confirmation and are reviewed by an administrator. Upon approval, your account and associated data will be permanently removed according to our data retention policies, subject to legal requirements.

Account Suspension

Administrators may suspend or terminate accounts that violate these terms, exhibit suspicious activity, or are involved in misuse of the platform.

6. Support & Ticket Policy

AgriCore provides a built-in ticketing system for reporting bugs, requesting features, or submitting general enquiries. When submitting a support ticket:

  • Provide clear, descriptive titles and details about the issue.
  • Select the appropriate ticket type (Bug, Feature Request, General Enquiry) and urgency level.
  • File attachments are limited to JPG, PNG, PDF, and TXT files (max 10 MB per file).
  • Ticket submissions are rate-limited to prevent abuse (5 tickets per 15-minute window).
  • You will receive notifications when your ticket status is updated or resolved.

Our support team aims to review and respond to tickets promptly. Critical bugs are prioritised over feature requests and general enquiries.
